# Closing your account

## Closing your account
You can ask to close your account in the app. The balance in every currency
must be zero first, so withdraw everything before asking.

## Open disputes and refunds
An account cannot be closed while it has a dispute open or under review, or a
refund waiting for approval.

## Data after closure
After the account is closed, Nimbus keeps your data for 5 years, as required
by law, and then deletes it.
