# Buying and selling bitcoin

## Buying and selling bitcoin
Verified customers can buy and sell bitcoin with reais in the app. Orders are
executed right away at the price shown on the screen.

## Price and spread
The price shown already includes a spread of 0.5%. There is no separate
trading fee.

## Trades are final
A trade cannot be cancelled, reversed or refunded after it is executed.

## Minimum trade
The minimum value for a trade is R$ 10.00.
