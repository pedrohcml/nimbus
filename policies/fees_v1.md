# Fees

## Deposits and withdrawals in reais
Deposits in BRL are free. Withdrawals in BRL are free.

## Bitcoin withdrawals
Bitcoin withdrawals pay a network fee to the miners who process the
transaction. The fee depends on how busy the network is and is shown before
the withdrawal is confirmed. Network fees are not refundable, because Nimbus
does not keep them.

## Card fees
The card issuance fee is R$ 15.00, charged once when the card is issued.
Card purchases have no extra Nimbus fee.

## Fees charged by mistake
A Nimbus fee charged by mistake can be refunded like any card charge.
