# Withdrawals and deposits

## Bitcoin confirmations
A bitcoin withdrawal is complete after 3 network confirmations. A new block
is added to the network about every 10 minutes on average, so this usually
takes around 30 minutes.

## Delays
A withdrawal that is still confirming more than 2 hours after it was created
is considered delayed. A transaction that is still pending more than 24
hours after it was created is also considered delayed. Support can escalate
delayed transactions to the operations team.

## Cancelling
A withdrawal cannot be cancelled or refunded after it is sent to the network.
