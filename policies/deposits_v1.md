# Deposits

## Depositing reais
Deposits in reais are made by PIX from a bank account in your own name. They
are usually credited within minutes. A deposit still pending after 24 hours
is considered delayed and can be sent to a human agent.

## Deposits from other people
Deposits from bank accounts in another person's name are not accepted. They
are returned to the sender within 5 business days.

## Depositing bitcoin
Bitcoin deposits are credited after 3 network confirmations. Send only
bitcoin, on the bitcoin network, to your Nimbus address. Other coins or
networks sent to that address may be lost.
