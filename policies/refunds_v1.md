# Refund policy

## What can be refunded
Card charges and Nimbus fees can be refunded once they are completed.
Deposits, withdrawals and trades cannot be refunded: crypto transfers are
final once they reach the network.

## Partial refunds
A refund can be for part of the amount. The total refunded for one
transaction can never exceed the amount of that transaction.

## Approval
When the total refunded for a transaction is up to R$ 300.00, the refund is
approved automatically. Above that, the support team reviews the request
before approving it. Splitting a refund into smaller requests does not avoid
the review: the total is what counts.

## When the money arrives
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. The card issuer
decides when the credit appears, so Nimbus cannot promise a date.
