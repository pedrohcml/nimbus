# Nimbus card

## Getting a card
The card can be requested in the app after identity verification. The
issuance fee is R$ 15.00, and the card arrives within 10 business days.

## Lost or stolen card
Block the card in the app right away. A blocked card cannot be used again.
A replacement card can be requested in the app and has the same issuance fee.

## Purchases abroad
The card works abroad. Purchases in other currencies are converted to reais
at the exchange rate of the day, plus the taxes required by Brazilian law.

## Declined purchases
A purchase is declined when the balance in reais is not enough, when it goes
above the daily card limit, or when the card is blocked.
