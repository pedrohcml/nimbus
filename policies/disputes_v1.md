# Dispute policy

## When to open a dispute
A dispute is a formal investigation of a transaction. It can be opened when a
transaction was not authorized by the customer, was charged twice, was paid
but the product or service was not received, or has the wrong amount.

## How disputes work
Only one dispute can be opened per transaction. Opening a dispute does not
move any money. The support team answers every dispute within 14 days of the
day it was opened.

## Possible outcomes
- Resolved in favor of the customer: the team returns the money or reverses
  the transaction.
- Resolved in favor of Nimbus: the charge stands.
- Rejected: the dispute did not have enough information to be investigated.

## Unauthorized transactions
When a customer does not recognize a transaction, the case is also sent to
the security team, which may protect the account while it investigates.
