# Account limits

## Daily withdrawal limits
Verified accounts can withdraw up to R$ 50,000.00 in reais and up to 2 BTC in
bitcoin per day. The limits reset at midnight, Brasília time.

## Deposit limits
There is no limit for deposits in reais from bank accounts in your own name.

## Raising your limits
You can ask for higher limits in the app. The team reviews each request and
may ask for proof of income before approving it.

## Card spending limit
The Nimbus card can spend up to R$ 10,000.00 per day. Purchases above the
daily limit are declined.
