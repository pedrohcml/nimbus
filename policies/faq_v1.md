# Frequently asked questions

## Which currencies does Nimbus support?
Nimbus accounts hold Brazilian reais (BRL) and bitcoin (BTC). Each account has
one wallet per currency, and a wallet balance can never be negative.

## What types of transactions are there?
- Deposit: money or bitcoin coming into your account.
- Withdrawal: money or bitcoin leaving your account, for example to an
  external bitcoin wallet.
- Trade: buying or selling bitcoin inside Nimbus.
- Card charge: a purchase made with the Nimbus card.
- Fee: a fee charged by Nimbus, such as the card issuance fee.

## What does each transaction status mean?
- Pending: received by Nimbus and waiting to be processed.
- Confirming: sent to the bitcoin network and waiting for confirmations.
- Completed: finished.
- Failed: did not go through.
- Reversed: was undone after it was processed.

## How do I find a transaction ID?
Every transaction has an ID that starts with "tx_", shown in the transaction
details in the app. Support needs this ID to look up a transaction: the chat
cannot search transactions by date or amount.

## How long does a bitcoin withdrawal take?
A bitcoin withdrawal is complete after 3 network confirmations. A new block is
added about every 10 minutes on average, so it usually takes around 30
minutes. Support can tell you how many confirmations a withdrawal already has.

## When is a transaction considered delayed?
A withdrawal still confirming more than 2 hours after it was created is
delayed. A transaction still pending more than 24 hours after it was created
is also delayed. Support can send delayed transactions to a human agent.

## Can I cancel a withdrawal?
No. A withdrawal cannot be cancelled or refunded after it is sent to the
network.

## What can be refunded?
Completed card charges and completed Nimbus fees. Deposits, withdrawals and
trades cannot be refunded. Transactions that are pending, confirming, failed
or reversed cannot be refunded either.

## Can I get a partial refund?
Yes. You can ask for part of the amount, and you can ask more than once. The
total refunded for one transaction can never exceed its amount. Refund
requests that were rejected do not count toward that total.

## Why is my refund under review?
When the total refunded for a transaction goes above R$ 500.00, the support
team reviews the request before approving it. Below that, refunds are approved
automatically. Splitting a refund into smaller requests does not avoid the
review: the total is what counts.

## What do the refund statuses mean?
- Under review: the support team is checking the request.
- Approved: the refund was approved. This does not mean the money has arrived.
- Executed: the refund was sent to the original payment method.
- Rejected: the refund was not approved.

## When will I receive my refund?
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. The card issuer
decides when the credit appears, so Nimbus cannot promise a date.

## What is the difference between a refund and a dispute?
A refund returns money: below the approval limit it is approved right away.
A dispute is a formal investigation of a transaction and does not move any
money when it is opened. Its outcome decides whether money is returned.

## How do I open a dispute?
Tell support the transaction ID and the reason. The accepted reasons are:
unauthorized transaction, duplicate charge, product or service not received,
wrong amount, or another reason. Only one dispute can be opened per
transaction.

## How long does a dispute take?
The support team answers every dispute within 14 days of the day it was
opened. A dispute can be open, under review, resolved in your favor, resolved
in favor of Nimbus, or rejected when there is not enough information.

## Can I ask for a refund while a dispute is open?
No. While a dispute on a transaction is open or under review, no refund can be
requested for it. The outcome of the dispute decides whether money is returned.

## Why do I have to confirm before a refund or dispute?
Before a refund or a dispute is created, support shows you the exact
transaction and amount and waits for you to reply "yes". Any other reply
cancels it, so nothing happens by mistake. Asking for the same refund twice in
the same conversation does not create a second one.

## What happens when I ask for a human?
Support hands the conversation to the human support team, which can assign
and close the case. A conversation has one case: if you give a new reason
later, it is added to the same case instead of opening another one.

## I don't recognize a transaction. What should I do?
Tell support right away. The case goes to the security team, and you can ask
for a dispute for an unauthorized transaction. Change your password and turn
on two-factor authentication.

## Will Nimbus ever ask for my password or card number?
Never. Nimbus never asks for passwords, two-factor codes or full card numbers.
Do not share them in the chat. Support does not copy them into your case.

## Can support see my balance or my verification status?
No. The support chat can look up transactions by ID, open disputes, request
refunds and hand the conversation to a human. Check your balance and your
identity verification status (pending, verified or rejected) in the app.

## Can I get help with another person's account?
No. Support only helps with the account that is signed in. To get help with
another account, sign in to it.

## Can support give me prices or investment advice?
No. Support has no market data and does not give financial or investment
advice.

## Are support actions recorded?
Yes. Every dispute, refund request and escalation made in the support chat is
recorded with its date and time.
