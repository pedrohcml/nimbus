# Account security

## What Nimbus never asks for
Nimbus never asks for passwords, two-factor codes or full card numbers, in
the chat or anywhere else. Customers should never share them.

## Suspected account takeover
If a customer does not recognize a transaction or thinks the account was
hacked, support escalates the case to the security team right away and
offers to open a dispute for unauthorized transactions. The customer should
change the password and turn on two-factor authentication.

## Other accounts
Support only helps with the account that is signed in. To get help with
another account, the customer must sign in to it.
