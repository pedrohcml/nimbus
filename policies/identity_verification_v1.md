# Identity verification

## Why we verify your identity
Brazilian law requires Nimbus to know who its customers are. Every account
goes through identity verification, also called KYC, before it can move money
out of Nimbus.

## Verification statuses
Verification can be pending (documents sent and waiting for review), verified
(the account is fully active) or rejected (the documents could not be
accepted). A rejected verification can be sent again with new documents.

## What you need
A photo of an official ID document with your CPF and a selfie taken in the
app. The name on the document must match the name on the account.

## How long verification takes
Most verifications are reviewed within 2 business days. Documents that are
blurry or cut off are rejected and must be sent again.

## What you can do before verification
Before the account is verified you can deposit reais and see your balance.
Withdrawals, trades and the Nimbus card are only available after
verification.
